Formula 1

May I introduce you to my best friend, the F1 timing overlay.

I started watching Formula 1 without understand a thing about cars or drivers, but I liked that it had so many numbers moving synchronizedly on the screen.

I've always had a weird obsession with numbers, progression, graphics and statistics (blame the tism) so, even if I still don't really like cars, the constant strategizing and analyzing of data everywhere has kept me hooked onto the races for about 3 years now
