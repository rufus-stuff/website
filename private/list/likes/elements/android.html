The Android OS

Some of you might remember that Google (before they became an AI company with AI powered by AI for AI with AI) used to do cool stuff like release innovative stuff, like Material design

When they released these alongside Android 5.0 Lollipop back in 2014 I became obsessed with the OS and everything that surrounded it. I remember studying the MD docs and trying to match all my schoolwork to them, and I started following Android releases and devices quite closely.

Back then I had motorola and LG as my fav brands, I had a Moto G (2nd gen) and the LG G6. Before becoming team Xiaomi for quite a while.

I loved exploring different Android skins and how each brand took the OS to different places. To this day I still consider it an awesome plan to go to phone stores just to try out new system versions on whatever device they have on display.
